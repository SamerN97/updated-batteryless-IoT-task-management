This directory contains solar irradiance data, captured in Hveraðerdi, Iceland, from September 2022 until August 2023. It was originally obtained and presented by Puluckul et al. [here](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10978303?casa_token=gyw0P3QKVKYAAAAA:P0sls88lLgYAS2p35Id1pGsLBwey9_1NjoKhQbO6Wxk0zpMuIIxAOCtzPS6k7-fuNC8Ik0ZZX7Za).
